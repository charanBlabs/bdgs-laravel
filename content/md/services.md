Express concierge setup for new Brilliant Directories sites. Go from zero to live in approximately 3 days with expert-guided configuration, business-model planning, and launch support. Best for owners who want a consultative setup — not a self-service install.

**Learn more:** https://bdgrowthsuite.com/setup

### 2. Hire a Developer

Dedicated Brilliant Directories developer embedded as a long-term team member. Ideal when you need ongoing development capacity without hiring in-house. Your developer learns your site, your business, and your roadmap.

**Learn more:** https://bdgrowthsuite.com/hire-developer

### 3. AI Development

Build AI into your directory — chatbots, smart search, automation workflows, and AI-powered member experiences. BD Growth Suite specializes in practical AI integrations that work within the Brilliant Directories platform.

**Learn more:** https://bdgrowthsuite.com/grow-with-ai

### 4. Custom Projects

One-shot scoped builds with fixed pricing. Custom widgets, page designs, integrations, and feature development tailored to your directory. Each project is scoped, quoted, and delivered by the BD Growth Suite development team.

### 5. Solutions Done-For-You

61+ pre-priced Brilliant Directories solutions ready to implement. Pick from SEO enhancements, lead generation tools, member profile upgrades, search improvements, page design packages, content features, integrations, and member management automation. We implement — you approve.

**Learn more:** https://bdgrowthsuite.com/solutions

### 6. Themes

6 premium industry-specific Brilliant Directories theme designs. Professionally designed starting points for directory businesses in specific verticals — faster launch with proven layouts and branding.

**Learn more:** https://bdgrowthsuite.com/themes

### 7. Maintenance Plans

Ongoing support, bug fixes, plugin updates, backups, and proactive monitoring for existing Brilliant Directories sites. Keeps your directory running smoothly so you can focus on growth.

**Learn more:** https://bdgrowthsuite.com/maintenance

### 8. Founder's Track (Apply Only)

High-touch, apply-only engagement combining strategy, development, and AI with direct involvement from Yakin Shah (founder). For directory founders who want a growth partner — not just a vendor. Application required.

**Learn more:** https://bdgrowthsuite.com/founders-track
