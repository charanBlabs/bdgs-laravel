# Decision 0002 — Webinar series labeling (Your First 100 Members)

**Date:** 2026-07-02  
**Status:** LOCKED (pending full Task 03 sign-off)  
**Owner:** Charan

## Context

MoM (Jul 1 review) required the "Your First 100 Members" webinar pair labeled consistently site-wide — either **Season 1 · Episode 1/2** or **Part 1 / Part 2**. Changes apply to **`/webinars/` only** — homepage CEO embed stays unchanged.

## Marketplace + BD blog verification (2026-07-02)

- [Business Labs marketplace](https://marketplace.brilliantdirectories.com/india/partner/business-labs): generic "Jason & Yakin webinar" — **no Part 1/2 or Episode labels**.
- [BD blog #205](https://www.brilliantdirectories.com/blog/your-first-100-members-simplify-your-directory-membership-website): official topic title only.
- [BD blog #213](https://www.brilliantdirectories.com/blog/your-first-100-members-build-a-high-converting-join-page): calls #205 the **"previous webinar"** and positions #213 as a **follow-up** — still no Part/Episode labels.

## Decision

**Do not use Part 1 / Part 2 or Season/Episode** on `/webinars/`.

Use **BD-aligned naming**:

| Label | Webinar | Official title (from BD blog) |
|-------|---------|-------------------------------|
| **First session** | #205 · Aug 13, 2025 | Simplify Your Directory + Membership Website |
| **Follow-up session** | #213 · Dec 3, 2025 | Build a High-Converting Join Page |

Both sit under a grouped **Your First 100 Members** series block, chronological order (#205 → #213).

**Rationale:** Matches how BD describes the pair in their own copy. Avoids inventing labels neither marketplace nor BD uses.

## Not chosen

- Part 1 / Part 2 — not used by BD or marketplace; partial "Part II" on old page was inconsistent.
- Season 1 · Episode 1/2 — no source uses this scheme.

## Scope

- **`/webinars/` only** — series grouping, session labels, nocookie embeds, internal CTAs.
- **Homepage** — no webinar-related changes (single CEO embed unchanged).
